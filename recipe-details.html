Vegetable Fried Rice

A quick, savory, and adaptable dish perfect for utilizing leftover rice and seasonal vegetables. High heat is essential for achieving the characteristic smoky flavor.

Prep Time 15 Min
Cook Time 10 Min
Servings 4
Difficulty Easy

Cooking Instructions

  1. 01

    Heat 1 tbsp of oil in a large wok or skillet over high heat. Pour in the beaten eggs and scramble quickly, then remove and set aside.

  2. 02

    Add the remaining oil to the same pan, then stir-fry the garlic and mixed vegetables for 2–3 minutes until just tender.

  3. 03

    Add the chilled rice, breaking up any clumps, and stir-fry for 4–5 minutes until the grains start to crisp at the edges.

  4. 04

    Return the scrambled egg to the pan, stir in the soy sauce and green onions, and toss everything together before serving hot.
